Retrieval limits:

- No complete unrestricted degree-20 certificate or publicly replayable
  Massri case archive was retrieved. The preprint's reported enumeration
  was not rerun in this task.
- Search snippets from social platforms, aggregators and recent purported
  “resolution” reports were leads only. They supplied no independent
  certificate used here. Absence from these searches is not an exhaustive
  literature or priority verdict.
- No new attempt was made to obtain ProofAtlas's missing source manuscript
  or contributor-only artifacts. Its unresolved overlap was already
  documented and remains open.
- Ghosh's disputed algebra and possible repair are assigned to a separate
  agent; this report does not certify either preprint's general theorem.
- No files in earlier frozen packages were modified.
- The degree-\(p+3\) queries did not identify an exact classification.
  Many returned irrelevant plane-curve papers by Casas-Alvero; those
  were not treated as evidence. This is a bounded retrieval statement.
