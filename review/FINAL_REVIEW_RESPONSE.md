# Response accompanying the completed eight-term candidate

24 September 2026.

The earlier concern was substantive: an incomplete frontier and an audit of another proof do not by themselves establish a sufficiently significant mathematical contribution. The research was therefore continued before preparing this paper.

The new candidate proves a stronger, complete sparsity result: a nontrivial characteristic-zero Casas–Alvero polynomial of degree twenty has at least eight centered monomials. The earlier open seven-term branches are all excluded. A separate coverage audit checks fourteen exact supports and nineteen compatible seed cases, with the complete earlier lower-term proof retained in Appendix A.

The new local arguments cover arbitrary ramification, coefficient degeneration, and algebraic-closure witness residues. The uniform second-jet theorem replaces a narrower ramification-field route in the main exposition. The final difficult support is settled through a separated-cluster reduction to the quartic case in characteristic seventeen and a simple-root obstruction modulo 17 squared.

Errors discovered during exploration were corrected before the final proof: a mixed-stratum valuation estimate was revised, and a quadratic second-jet calculation was rebuilt with the previously omitted degree-nineteen term. Superseded probes are labelled explicitly; only the corrected proofs and arithmetic checks enter the final case assembly.

The paper separates the mathematical proof, finite exact calculations, internal argument review, literature comparison and artifact integrity. Novelty is not inferred from a failed search. The primary-source comparisons and unresolved equivalence checks are recorded in FINAL_RESEARCH_LITERATURE.md. The significance assessment is in EIGHT_TERM_ASSESSMENT.md; no strong three-star rating is asserted.

The requested review should assess the eight-term theorem and its local methods as a new candidate. It should not treat the package as a full solution of the degree-twenty or general conjecture, an external referee acceptance, or a publication.
