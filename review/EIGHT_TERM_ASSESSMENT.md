## What changed mathematically

The earlier seven-term bound plus a shrinking list of seven-term supports was a useful but incomplete extension at the next threshold. The present proof excludes every seven-term support after an exhaustive, independently checked inventory. It yields the concise statement that every nontrivial degree-20 CA polynomial has at least eight total monomials after centering at its mean. That statement is strictly stronger and easier for other researchers to use.

The evidence is more than a further census. The direct second-jet theorem controls arbitrary intermediate coefficients in a fixed prime-17 stratum and absorbs two former arguments plus their missing boundary. The mixed-unit proofs force exact derivative-root collisions by complete cluster multiplicities. The quadratic second-jet proof determines its residue field before invoking a nonsquare obstruction and proves the required precision over arbitrarily ramified extensions. The remaining row-2 proof uses a good-characteristic quartic cluster argument and an outside simple-root obstruction. These are coherent local mechanisms with distinct uses in the global assembly.

The correction to the quadratic calculation is also material: the unit multiplier \(\binom{19}{2}\) contributes at first order. The final proof retains it, giving the discriminant-3 system, while the superseded probe is explicitly invalid evidence. Honest correction history and independent audits improve inspectability; they are not additional novelty claims.

## Why the significance claim still has limits

The theorem concerns one fixed degree and sparsity. It does not prove the degree-20 conjecture, an infinite new family of degrees, or an unrestricted characteristic-17 branch exclusion. Dense candidates remain. The degree-20 problem is prominent, but proximity to a famous conjecture alone does not make every restriction on its hypothetical counterexamples highly significant.

The classical tools are established. The potential originality lies in the exact local obstructions and complete eight-term consequence, not in Newton polygons, Hensel reasoning, the unit-Jacobian bound, support stratification or the existing determinant tests. Some local statements permit many nonzero coefficients, which strengthens their conceptual content, but their prime, degree and residue hypotheses must remain visible.

The finite certificate package and independent arithmetic reconstruction provide meaningful assurance. They do not mechanically verify all valuation arguments, and internal agents are not external referees. The full-text NTHU gap and unresolved exact equivalence to the ProofAtlas route also prevent an unconditional priority declaration. The fresh bounded search found no exact match; absence of a search hit is not a proof of novelty.

My calibrated position is therefore: **a stronger and plausibly original specialist contribution, with a credible publication case and a materially improved significance argument; a strong three-star assessment remains unconfirmed.** This is a substantive improvement over the earlier finite-frontier-only assessment, without asserting an externally validated level that the evidence cannot supply.
