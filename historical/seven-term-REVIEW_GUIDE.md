# Review guide

Read PAPER.pdf first. Its principal seven-term theorem is Theorem S; section
S.8 lists the complete finite-certificate dependency path. The following
support sections prove six new global exclusions and list eight survivors.
The local marked-factor section deliberately leaves scalar nonvanishing open.

Read review/REVIEW_RESPONSE.md for changes, significance limits, and review
questions; review/NOVELTY.md records the precise source comparisons.
review/FINAL_ASSESSMENT.md is an internal editorial assessment, not a grade
from an external assessment panel.

For old exploratory routes and the 79-of-240 row-9 coverage, use REPORT.pdf
and historical/portable-REVIEW_GUIDE.md. Those files are historical evidence,
not the final scientific narrative. Default replay does not regenerate the
largest census. Use README.md and PORTABILITY_CHANGES.md for exact commands.
