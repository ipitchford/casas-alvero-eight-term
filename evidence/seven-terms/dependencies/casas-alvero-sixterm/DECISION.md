# Research assessment after the bounded three-support investigation

The bounded investigation is complete: A and B are excluded; C remains. The seven-term target was not achieved.

The A argument is the strongest development. It turns a nonempty reduction locus, which initially blocked the planned method, into an exact root-collision constraint in characteristic zero. That is a useful mechanism for further research. The elementary principle that a simple residual root cannot split into two integral roots is established mathematics; no novelty claim is made for that principle. The candidate contribution is its complete application to this support.

The remaining C family is a more concrete target than the original three-family list: it has two possible normalized residue coefficient patterns, with six possible marked-root assignments. Some force exact witness collisions. A remaining assignment allows splitting inside a multiple residue-root cluster, so the simple-root argument cannot close it. Those structural facts give a reason for a focused follow-up, but not evidence that the full conjecture is now tractable. Solving C would prove a seven-term lower bound, still far short of unrestricted degree 20 or arbitrary degree.

Further work should start from the written C branch equations, with explicit nonzero coefficients and valuation assumptions. It should seek a uniform obstruction for one of the exact-collision classes or a valuation identity that controls the multiple-root cluster. Repeating unstructured larger Groebner calculations or unramified modular lifts would not settle the identified gap. The bounded degree-31 pilots and timed-out membership extraction attempts are retained as inconclusive discovery records; no mathematical conclusion rests on their failure.

This is a specialist partial result worth preserving and exposing to mathematical review. Its publication significance is unconfirmed. The bounded prior-art search did not locate these exact exclusions, but a public overview refers to undisclosed degree-20 support computations. Those formulas and certificates were unavailable, so priority remains unresolved. No claim that these are the first exclusions, and no claim of a major advance toward the complete conjecture, is warranted by the current evidence.

The previous frozen packages and archives remain unchanged. This round authorizes no submission, publication, or external outreach, and none occurred.
