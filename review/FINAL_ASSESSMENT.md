specialist result. The additional complete exclusions of exact seven-term

## What the extra frontier exclusions change

Assuming the separate coverage and arithmetic audits confirm all six new
exclusions, the permitted exact seven-term frontier shrinks from fourteen
supports to eight under the previously established sieve. This is a
stronger conditional classification of the minimum remaining term count.
The denominator fourteen is the already filtered frontier, not the entire
degree-20 incidence problem. It should not be presented as a percentage of
the conjecture solved.

The most useful presentation is a corollary listing the eight remaining
exact deficiency sets, accompanied by a table assigning each removed set
to its exhaustive reduction alternatives and the certificate excluding
each alternative. In particular, a support allowing both row 8 and row 9
is removed only after both alternatives are closed. A prime-field search
alone would not do this: coverage over the algebraic closure and the
possible residue-zero active coefficients must be part of the relevant
theorems.

The existing seven-term theorem is the main global contribution. Six more
supports improve it quantitatively but do not change its qualitative
ceiling. A completed unrestricted residue-branch exclusion, a uniform new
class of degrees, or a reusable obstruction with materially distinct
consequences could support a stronger significance judgment. This revision
does not need to suggest that it has achieved one of those results.
